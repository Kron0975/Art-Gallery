About the Exhibition

Welcome to Art & Imagination 2026, a unique exhibition showcasing creative artworks from talented artists around the world. Visitors can explore paintings, sculptures, and digital art pieces.

Theme

In this Exhibition we are gonna recreate the evergreen illusionistic ceiling painting also known as illusionism.

Illusionism in art history refers to the use of highly precise artistic techniques to create a visual representation that perfectly mimics the three-dimensional world on a flat, two-dimensional surface.

Note : The artists can also exhibit their other artforms, the theme is not compulsory.
